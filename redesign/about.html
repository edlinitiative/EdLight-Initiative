The Evolution of EdLight

EARLY 2022

The First Spark

Founded with a singular vision: to bring world-class educational infrastructure to Port-au-Prince. We started with 20 students in a makeshift lab, focusing on core programming fundamentals.

MID 2023

Institutional Expansion

Launch of the Nexus platform, connecting students with global mentorship. EdLight Academy moves into its permanent campus, a hub of modern Haitian architecture and digital innovation.

PRESENT DAY

A Multi-Disciplinary Ecosystem

Today, EdLight encompasses Labs, the ESLP leadership program, and a nationwide network of alumni building the next phase of Haiti's digital economy.

Our R&D Methodology

We employ a rigorous four-stage lifecycle designed to ensure every innovation is sustainable, culturaly relevant, and scalable.

01

Synthesis

Identifying core structural bottlenecks through ethnographic and data-driven immersion in Haitian markets.

02

Prototyping

Rapid engineering of MVP (Minimum Viable Products) using lean infrastructure and edge computing.

03

Stress Testing

Deploying in controlled 'Real-World' environments to test resilience under local power and data constraints.

04

Integration

Finalizing enterprise-grade protocols and integrating successful solutions into the EdLight Ecosystem.
